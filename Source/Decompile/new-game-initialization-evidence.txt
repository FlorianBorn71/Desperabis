the port's explicit integration of known part starts, not a decoded
chapter-resource row. New Game resets progress before normal StartLevel.

accepted New Game does not promise rollback of old progress on failure.
Host debug/audio preferences survive; host cheat loadouts apply as usual.

exposes all three for reconstruction/testing rather than inventing unlock
state. Native helper 0000:81F2..8294 reads alternating chapter/game-name
rows from DS:05C0; exact label metadata and carousel art remain unported.
